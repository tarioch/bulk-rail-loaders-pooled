Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-16
  Features:
    - Loading stations load exactly the items and amounts of a Cybersyn 2 delivery.
  Graphics:
    - The unloader grate fits the 4x4 silo, has a wall around the pit and is drawn under the rails.
    - The loader is drawn at double resolution with a brushed-metal frame and a softer shadow.
    - The vertical loader has a ribbed panel over its track opening.
  Bugfixes:
    - Silos can be placed over ore.
  Info:
    - First release of the fork under the name bulk-rail-loaders-pooled-plus.
    - Stations in a save made with the original mod or the Unofficial 2.1 Fix are re-registered on first load (from the Unofficial 2.1 Fix by kfsone). Station mode resets to Load.
    - Remote interface mbrlp with set_station_mode and get_station_mode (from the Unofficial 2.1 Fix by kfsone).
